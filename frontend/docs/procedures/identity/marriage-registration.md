# Marriage Registration (Civil / Non-Muslim)

## Overview

Marriage registration is the official process of registering a legal marriage for non-Muslim couples in Malaysia through the National Registration Department (JPN).

---

## Who Can Apply?

- Malaysian citizens
- Permanent residents (where applicable)
- Couples who meet the legal age requirement
- Couples legally eligible to marry under Malaysian law

---

## Required Documents

Typically required documents include:

- MyKad or passport
- Birth certificate (if required)
- Passport-sized photographs (if required)
- Supporting documents for foreign citizens (if applicable)

Requirements may vary depending on individual circumstances.

---

## Fees

Registration fees vary depending on the application and location.

Please refer to the official JPN website for the latest fees.

---

## Processing Time

Processing time depends on document verification and appointment availability.

---

## Where to Apply

- National Registration Department (JPN)

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Check your eligibility.
2. Prepare all required documents.
3. Submit the marriage application.
4. Attend the scheduled appointment.
5. Complete the marriage registration.
6. Receive the Marriage Certificate.

---

## What to Bring

✅ Original identification documents

✅ Supporting documents

✅ Appointment confirmation (if applicable)

---

## Before You Go

✔ Check that all documents are complete.

✔ Confirm your appointment date and time.

✔ Arrive early at the JPN office.

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ Bringing incomplete documents.

❌ Missing the scheduled appointment.

❌ Incorrect personal information on the application.

---

## 💡 StepMY Tips

⭐ Make photocopies of all important documents.

⭐ Check that both applicants' details match across all documents.

⭐ Save your appointment confirmation.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can foreigners register a marriage in Malaysia?

It depends on eligibility and additional documentation requirements.

### Can I reschedule my appointment?

This depends on JPN procedures.

### Do both applicants need to be present?

Yes, both applicants are generally required to attend the registration.

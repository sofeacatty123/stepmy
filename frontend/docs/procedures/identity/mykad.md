# MyKad Application & Replacement

## Overview

MyKad is Malaysia's official national identity card issued by the National Registration Department (JPN). It is required for identification and many government and banking services.

---

## Who Can Apply?

- Malaysian citizens
- First-time MyKad applicants (eligible age)
- Citizens replacing a lost, damaged, or outdated MyKad
- Citizens updating personal information

---

## Required Documents

Depending on your application:

### First-Time Application
- Birth Certificate
- Parent or guardian's MyKad (if applicable)

### Replacement
- Existing MyKad (if damaged)
- Police report (if required for loss)
- Supporting documents (if updating personal details)

---

## Fees

Fees depend on the type of application.

Examples include:
- First-time application
- Lost MyKad
- Damaged MyKad
- Change of information

Refer to the official JPN website for the latest fees.

---

## Processing Time

Usually completed within several working days, depending on the application type and JPN branch.

---

## Where to Apply

- National Registration Department (JPN)
- Selected Urban Transformation Centres (UTC)

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Prepare all required documents.
2. Visit the nearest JPN office or UTC.
3. Take a queue number.
4. Submit your documents.
5. Pay the required fee (if applicable).
6. Complete fingerprint and photo verification.
7. Collect your new MyKad.

---

## What to Bring

✅ Existing MyKad (if replacing)

✅ Supporting documents

✅ Payment method

---

## Before You Go

✔ Check JPN operating hours.

✔ Prepare photocopies if needed.

✔ Ensure your personal information is correct.

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ Forgetting supporting documents.

❌ Bringing damaged or incomplete paperwork.

❌ Visiting the wrong JPN branch for certain services.

---

## 💡 StepMY Tips

⭐ Visit early in the morning to reduce waiting time.

⭐ Keep digital copies of important documents.

⭐ Double-check spelling before submitting forms.

---

## FAQ

### Can I replace a lost MyKad?

Yes, replacement applications can be made at JPN. Additional requirements or fees may apply.

### Can I update my address?

Yes. Supporting documents may be required.

### Can someone else apply on my behalf?

This depends on the type of application. Check with JPN for the latest policy.

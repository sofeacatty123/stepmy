# Birth Certificate Application & Replacement

## Overview

A birth certificate is an official document issued by the National Registration Department (JPN) that records a person's birth. This guide explains how to apply for a certified copy or replace a lost birth certificate.

---

## Who Can Apply?

- The certificate owner
- Parents or legal guardians
- Immediate family members (subject to JPN requirements)
- An authorised representative (where permitted)

---

## Required Documents

Depending on your application:

### Applicant

- MyKad
- Existing birth certificate (if available)

### Representative

- Applicant's MyKad copy
- Representative's MyKad
- Authorisation letter (if required)

---

## Fees

Fees vary depending on the type of application.

Please refer to the official JPN website for the latest fee schedule.

---

## Processing Time

Processing time depends on:

- Type of application
- JPN branch
- Document verification

---

## Where to Apply

- National Registration Department (JPN)
- Selected Urban Transformation Centres (UTC)

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Prepare all required documents.
2. Visit the nearest JPN office or UTC.
3. Take a queue number.
4. Submit your application.
5. Pay the required fee.
6. Wait for processing.
7. Collect your birth certificate.

---

## What to Bring

✅ MyKad

✅ Supporting documents

✅ Payment method

---

## Before You Go

✔ Check JPN operating hours.

✔ Confirm which JPN branch offers the service.

✔ Prepare photocopies if required.

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ Bringing incomplete documents.

❌ Forgetting your MyKad.

❌ Applying at the wrong counter.

---

## 💡 StepMY Tips

⭐ Visit early to avoid long queues.

⭐ Bring both original and photocopies of important documents.

⭐ Keep the receipt until you receive your certificate.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I replace a lost birth certificate?

Yes. Replacement applications can be made through JPN.

### Can someone else apply on my behalf?

Yes, subject to JPN requirements and supporting documents.

### Can I apply at any JPN office?

Most applications can be submitted at JPN offices, but some services may only be available at selected branches.

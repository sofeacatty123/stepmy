# Malaysian Passport Renewal

## Overview

A Malaysian International Passport allows Malaysian citizens to travel internationally.

This guide explains how to renew your passport online or at an Immigration Office.

---

## Who Can Apply?

- Malaysian citizens
- Existing passport holders
- Passport has expired, is expiring soon, or has no remaining pages

---

## Required Documents

- MyKad
- Current passport
- Birth certificate (if under 18 years old)

---

## Application Methods

### Option 1 — Online

Suitable if:

- Current passport is still available
- Passport is not damaged
- Passport is not lost

### Option 2 — Immigration Office

Required if:

- Passport is lost
- Passport is damaged
- Applicant is not eligible for online renewal

---

## Fees

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| Adult (13–59 years) | RM200 |
| Children (12 years and below) | RM100 |
| Senior Citizen (60+) | RM100 |
| OKU | Free |

---

## Processing Time

Online:
Usually several working days after approval and collection. Processing times can vary by branch and demand.

Walk-in:
Depends on the Immigration Office and queue.

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Prepare all required documents.
2. Choose online or counter application.
3. Complete the application.
4. Pay the required fee.
5. Attend the Immigration Office if required.
6. Collect your new passport.

---

## What to Wear

✅ Wear clothing that covers your shoulders.

Avoid:

- Sleeveless tops
- Hats
- Clothing that may not meet passport photo requirements

---

## Before You Go

✔ Bring your MyKad.

✔ Bring your existing passport.

✔ Double-check that your passport is not damaged.

✔ Keep your payment receipt.

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ Bringing a damaged passport for online renewal.

❌ Forgetting your MyKad.

❌ Wearing clothing unsuitable for passport photographs.

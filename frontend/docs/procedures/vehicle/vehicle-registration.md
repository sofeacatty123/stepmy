# Vehicle Registration

## Overview

Vehicle registration is the process of officially registering a new vehicle with the Road Transport Department (JPJ) before it can be legally driven on Malaysian roads.

---

## Who Can Apply?

- Malaysian citizens
- Permanent residents
- Companies purchasing new vehicles
- Individuals registering a newly purchased vehicle

---

## Required Documents

The required documents may include:

- MyKad or passport
- Proof of vehicle ownership
- Vehicle purchase invoice
- Insurance certificate
- PUSPAKOM inspection report (if applicable)
- Supporting documents requested by JPJ

---

## Fees

Registration fees vary depending on:

- Vehicle type
- Engine capacity
- Registration number selection
- Other applicable JPJ charges

Please refer to the official JPJ website for the latest fees.

---

## Processing Time

Processing time depends on:

- Document verification
- Vehicle inspection (if required)
- JPJ branch workload

---

## Where to Apply

- Road Transport Department (JPJ)
- Authorised vehicle dealers (for new vehicles)

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Purchase vehicle insurance.
2. Complete vehicle ownership documents.
3. Prepare all required documents.
4. Visit JPJ or allow the authorised dealer to register the vehicle.
5. Pay the registration fees.
6. Receive the vehicle registration details.
7. Collect your registration documents and number plate (if applicable).

---

## What to Bring

✅ MyKad

✅ Vehicle purchase documents

✅ Insurance documents

✅ Supporting documents

---

## Before You Go

✔ Check all vehicle information.

✔ Ensure insurance has been activated.

✔ Confirm whether an appointment is required.

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ Missing ownership documents.

❌ Insurance not yet activated.

❌ Incorrect vehicle details.

---

## 💡 StepMY Tips

⭐ Most authorised vehicle dealers will handle the registration process for new vehicles.

⭐ Double-check your engine and chassis numbers before submission.

⭐ Keep copies of all registration documents.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to visit JPJ?

Not always. Many authorised vehicle dealers complete the registration on behalf of buyers.

### Can I choose my vehicle registration number?

Yes. JPJ offers registration number selection and bidding services.

### Is insurance required before registration?

Yes. Vehicle insurance is generally required before registration can be completed.

# PUSPAKOM Vehicle Inspection

## Overview

PUSPAKOM (Pusat Pemeriksaan Kenderaan Berkomputer) is Malaysia's authorised vehicle inspection company. Vehicle inspections are required for certain situations such as ownership transfers, imported vehicles, commercial vehicles, and special vehicle modifications.

---

## Who Needs a PUSPAKOM Inspection?

A PUSPAKOM inspection may be required for:

- Vehicle ownership transfer
- Imported vehicles
- Commercial vehicles
- Modified vehicles
- Special inspections requested by JPJ

---

## Required Documents

Prepare the following documents before your appointment:

- MyKad or passport
- Vehicle Registration Certificate (VOC / Geran)
- Appointment confirmation
- Supporting documents (if applicable)

Additional documents may be required depending on the inspection type.

---

## Inspection Fees

Inspection fees depend on:

- Inspection type
- Vehicle category
- Additional inspection services

Refer to the official PUSPAKOM website for the latest fee schedule.

---

## Processing Time

The inspection itself usually takes around 30–60 minutes, depending on the vehicle condition and customer queue.

---

## Where to Go

Visit any authorised PUSPAKOM inspection centre in Malaysia.

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Book an inspection appointment.
2. Prepare all required documents.
3. Bring the vehicle to the selected PUSPAKOM centre.
4. Complete the vehicle inspection.
5. Receive the inspection result.
6. Use the inspection report for your JPJ application if required.

---

## Inspection Checklist

Before your appointment, ensure:

✅ Vehicle registration documents are available.

✅ Vehicle number plate is clearly visible.

✅ Lights, brakes, tyres and horn are functioning properly.

✅ Chassis and engine numbers are readable.

---

## Common Reasons for Inspection Failure

❌ Faulty brakes

❌ Damaged tyres

❌ Broken lights

❌ Illegal vehicle modifications

❌ Engine or chassis number cannot be identified

---

## 💡 StepMY Tips

⭐ Book your appointment early to reduce waiting time.

⭐ Wash your vehicle before inspection.

⭐ Check tyres, lights and brakes before visiting.

⭐ Bring both original and photocopies of important documents.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is an appointment required?

Yes. Appointments are recommended and may be required depending on the inspection centre.

### How long does the inspection take?

Most inspections take around 30–60 minutes, depending on the vehicle and queue.

### What happens if my vehicle fails?

You will receive information about the issues that need to be fixed. After repairs, you may need to schedule another inspection.

# UPU Online Application

## Overview

UPU Online is Malaysia's central online admission system for applications to public universities (UA), polytechnics, community colleges, and selected public institutions. Students can use UPU Online to apply for diploma, foundation, certificate, and bachelor's degree programmes.

---

## Who Can Apply?

Applicants who meet the eligibility requirements, including:

- SPM graduates
- STPM graduates
- Matriculation students
- Foundation students
- Diploma holders
- Equivalent qualification holders accepted by UPU

Eligibility depends on the programme and institution.

---

## Requirements

Before applying, prepare:

- MyKad number
- Academic examination results
- Active email address
- Mobile phone number
- Required supporting documents (if requested)

---

## Application Fee

Some application categories may require the purchase of a PIN number before submitting an application.

Refer to the official UPU Online website for the latest fees and payment methods.

---

## Application Period

Applications are only open during the official UPU Online intake period announced each year.

Always check the latest application schedule before applying.

---

## How to Apply

1. Visit the official UPU Online website.
2. Log in using your MyKad number.
3. Complete your personal information.
4. Select your preferred programmes.
5. Review all information carefully.
6. Submit your application.
7. Save or print the application confirmation.

---

## Required Documents

Depending on your application:

- MyKad
- Academic transcripts
- Examination results
- Supporting certificates (if applicable)

---

## Before You Submit

✔ Ensure your personal information is correct.

✔ Arrange programme choices carefully.

✔ Double-check all uploaded information.

✔ Save your application confirmation.

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ Missing the application deadline.

❌ Entering incorrect examination results.

❌ Choosing programmes without checking eligibility.

❌ Forgetting to save the confirmation slip.

---

## 💡 StepMY Tips

⭐ Research each programme before selecting it.

⭐ Arrange your programme choices according to your interests and qualifications.

⭐ Keep your application number and confirmation for future reference.

⭐ Monitor announcements regularly after submitting your application.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I edit my application after submitting it?

UPU may allow updates during the application period. Refer to official announcements for the latest policy.

### Can I apply for multiple programmes?

Yes. Applicants may select multiple programme choices according to UPU guidelines.

### How do I check my application result?

Results can be checked through the official UPU Online portal when released.
